Privacy Policy

How Novation Moving Broker collects, uses, and protects your information

Last Updated: July 24, 2026  |  Effective Date: July 24, 2026

Novation Moving Broker ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") operates as a moving broker and medical courier service based in Tampa, Florida. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have regarding your information.

By using our website at novationmovingbroker.com or contacting us for services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you request a quote, contact us, or book a service, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Pickup and delivery addresses (ZIP codes and city)
  • Service type requested (moving, medical courier, cargo delivery, etc.)
  • Preferred service date
  • Any additional details you choose to share in message fields

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring website or search engine
  • Device type (desktop, tablet, mobile)

1.3 Medical & Sensitive Information

For medical courier and pharmacy delivery services, we may receive information about the nature of items being transported (e.g., prescription medications, lab specimens, medical supplies). We do not collect, store, or share patient health information (PHI) beyond what is strictly necessary to complete a delivery, and we handle all such information with care consistent with applicable privacy laws.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to quote requests and inquiries
  • Schedule, coordinate, and fulfill moving and courier services
  • Communicate with you about your service (confirmations, updates, follow-ups)
  • Send you promotional communications (only with your consent)
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Prevent fraud and ensure safety

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

3.1 Service Partners

As a moving broker, we may share your service request details with licensed and vetted third-party carriers or drivers who fulfill your move or delivery. These partners are required to handle your information securely and use it only to complete your service.

3.2 Service Providers

We use third-party tools to operate our business, including:

  • Formspree — to receive and route quote form submissions
  • Google Maps — for location and mapping features on our website
  • Cloudflare — for website hosting, performance, and security

These providers only access your data to perform services on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality agreements.

3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government authority, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Novation Moving Broker, our customers, or the public.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, provide our services, and comply with applicable laws. Quote request data is typically retained for up to 2 years. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

5. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at Novationmultiservices@gmail.com.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience and analyze website traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some functionality of the website.

We currently do not use third-party advertising cookies or sell data to advertisers.

7. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These include HTTPS encryption, secure form submission, and access controls on our systems.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (such as Google Maps). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies separately.

10. Florida Privacy Rights

As a Florida-based company serving Florida residents, we comply with applicable Florida privacy laws. Florida residents may contact us to exercise their data rights as described in Section 5 above.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our website or services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
