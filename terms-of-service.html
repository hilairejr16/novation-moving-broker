1. Our Role as a Moving Broker

Novation Moving Broker operates as a licensed moving broker. This means we arrange for transportation of your household goods, commercial goods, or medical items by connecting you with licensed, insured, and vetted third-party carriers and drivers.

Important: Novation Moving Broker is not a motor carrier and will not physically transport your household goods. The company that performs your actual move may be a different company than Novation Moving Broker. All carriers we work with are independently licensed and insured.

For medical courier and cargo van delivery services, Novation Moving Broker may operate directly or through contracted delivery professionals.
